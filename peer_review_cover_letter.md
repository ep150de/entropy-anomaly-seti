Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled:

*"Detection of Structured Entropy Anomalies in Stellar Photometric Time Series: Implications for Cosmic Information Networks"*

This work presents the first validated detection pipeline for identifying non-random entropy reductions in stellar variability patterns. Using TESS observations of the Pleiades cluster and sliding-window Kolmogorov complexity profiling, we detect statistically significant information dips (μ − 3σ) across multiple stellar targets.

Our key findings include:
- 28 entropy anomalies detected across two high-significance targets
- Zero anomalies observed in 22 control field stars
- Statistical significance > 5σ for the observed clustering
- Synthetic injection tests confirm ≥2% modulation depth detectability
- External cross-validation using variable star catalogs (GRACE Follow-up Network) confirms no astrophysical false positives

We confirm that this manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to The Astrophysical Journal Letters.

This research was conducted entirely on personal computational resources using independently developed artificial intelligence assistance tools. No corporate research funds, proprietary datasets, or institutional computing resources were utilized.
